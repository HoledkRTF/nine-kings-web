# Project Agents & Version History

## Recent Codenames
* **Gothic-Ascendance** (Tag: `Gothic-Ascendance`) — Comprehensive visual overhaul: beveled flagstone highway pavers, dynamic persistent tread tracks and charnel ruts, colossal horned demon ram with heavy caterpillar treads, rotating barded cavalry with couched tournament lances, volumetric drifting fog banks, floating soul wisps, cinematic edge vignette, and ornate gothic filigree HUD.

* **Autonomous-Vanguard** (Tag: `Autonomous-Vanguard`) — Minion AI decoupled from cursor tracking: minions maintain pure autonomous formation escort and independent aggro intercepts without following the mouse pointer.
* **Relic-Sanctuary** (Tag: `Relic-Sanctuary`) — Layered gothic battlefield terrain with cobblestone highways, ruined mausoleums, iron cemetery fences, and rolling atmospheric ground mist. 2.5x enemy density boost with defensive garrison AI clustering around Holy Relic Vaults. Shattering divine wards awards permanent upgrades (extra weapon sockets, minion cap boosts, and relic combat stats).
