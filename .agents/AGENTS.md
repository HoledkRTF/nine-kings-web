# Project Agents & Version History

## Recent Codenames
* **Shadow-Infiltrator** (Tag: `Shadow-Infiltrator`) — Faithful, zero-dependency Pass & Play clone of `findtheimposter.com/game`: Classic Imposter and Undercover game modes; 45 curated categories with hundreds of word pairs; synthesized Web Audio API sound effects; deterministic SVG character avatars; 3D card-flip reveal phase; customizable discussion timer with speaking turn spotlight; pass-the-device secret ballot voting with Guardian shield protection, tie-breaking runoffs, Mr. White guess screen, and Last Chance imposter guess; celebratory victory screen with confetti and persistent scoreboard.
* **Imperial-Dominion** (Tag: `Imperial-Dominion`) — Studio-grade 9 Kings gameplay visuals and tactile HUD overhaul powered by specialized gamedev skills (`deckbuilder-ui`, `game-juice`, `game-feel`, `pixel-art-gamedev`, `create-game-assets`): authentic top HUD with gold currency (`✦ 25 +5`), breaches (`0 / 3`), left-side runic spell medallions, and milestone timeline track (`YEAR 1`).
* **Verdant-Elegance** (Tag: `Verdant-Elegance`) — Studio-grade visual overhaul of 9 Kings procedural canvas environment matching authentic Steam art: hand-chiseled rounded cobblestone wall with mortar courses and 3D fortress crenellations; multi-layered conifer spruce forest with drooping scalloped needle tiers.
* **Synchronized-Territory** (Tag: `Synchronized-Territory`) — Synchronized world camera transform locking 3x3 and 4x4 meadow grids directly to the procedural grass pasture.
* **Sapphire-Territory** (Tag: `Sapphire-Territory`) — Expandable 9 Kings territory grid system flat on the procedural meadow: starts as authentic 3x3 (9 diamond plots) and expands to 4x4 (16 diamond plots) with smooth golden border shimmer animation.
