# Project Agents & Version History

## Recent Codenames
* **Pixel-Phalanx** (Tag: `Pixel-Phalanx`) — Authentic retro pixel art rendering for all 6 minions with 16x16 / 24x24 multi-frame animation matrices, rasterized offscreen canvas caching, and razor-sharp nearest-neighbor smoothing.
* **Horde-Vanguard** (Tag: `Horde-Vanguard`) — Autonomous minion AI overhaul: strict citadel exclusion preventing minions from hiding under hull, boids inter-minion separation, role-based escort formations (front bone phalanx vanguard, rearguard acolytes, flanking sweepers), autonomous enemy aggro pursuit, bloated husk suicide detonations, and player click targeting.
* **Dread-Velocity** (Tag: `Dread-Velocity`) — Visual sprites overhaul (articulated crawler legs, ribcage crossbeams, glowing soul furnace, horned bone ram, procedural minion & crusade enemy sprites, grimdark terrain with tombstones, particle splatters) and 2.5x citadel speed & acceleration boost.
* **Bone-Dreadnought** (Tag: `Bone-Dreadnought`) — Full playable implementation of Ossuary Citadel with 360° damage-typed sockets, dual-corpse economy, 6-unit minion horde vanguard, active necromancy spells, and crusade wave director promoted to main.
* **Grave-Chariot** (Tag: `Grave-Chariot`) — Initial design specification for Ossuary Citadel.
