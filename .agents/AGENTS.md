# Project Agents & Version History

## Recent Codenames
* **Soul-Harvester** (Tag: `Soul-Harvester`) — Soul EXP bar progression with 3-card rogue-lite level-up drafting, unlockable minion tiers summoned on keys 1–6, and Carrion Scavenger perk enabling minions to autonomously harvest ground corpses.
* **Autonomous-Vanguard** (Tag: `Autonomous-Vanguard`) — Minion AI decoupled from cursor tracking: minions maintain pure autonomous formation escort and independent aggro intercepts without following the mouse pointer.
* **Relic-Sanctuary** (Tag: `Relic-Sanctuary`) — Layered gothic battlefield terrain with cobblestone highways, ruined mausoleums, iron cemetery fences, and rolling atmospheric ground mist. 2.5x enemy density boost with defensive garrison AI clustering around Holy Relic Vaults. Shattering divine wards awards permanent upgrades (extra weapon sockets, minion cap boosts, and relic combat stats).
* **Charnel-Vanguard** (Tag: `Charnel-Vanguard`) — Standoff perimeter distance expansion (85px minimum citadel hull clearance, 90–150px wide escort formations), obstacle tangential steering around citadel, autonomous 550px aggro intercept pursuit, boosted minion movement speed, and upscaled pixel art sprites with directional flipping and shadows.
* **Pixel-Phalanx** (Tag: `Pixel-Phalanx`) — Authentic retro pixel art rendering for all 6 minions with 16x16 / 24x24 multi-frame animation matrices, rasterized offscreen canvas caching, and razor-sharp nearest-neighbor smoothing.
