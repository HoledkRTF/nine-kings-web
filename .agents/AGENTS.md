# Project Agents & Version History

## Recent Codenames
* **Purging-Crusade** (Tag: `Purging-Crusade`) — Anti-snowball hardcore overhaul: minion soul tether lifespans with visual expiration flicker, swarm-triggered Crusade Wrath escalation, Paladin greatsword cleave arcs, Silver Cavalry trample charges, High Inquisitor Radiant Sunburst anti-swarm purges, and Trebuchet Consecration shells.
* **Dread-Necromancer** (Tag: `Dread-Necromancer`) — Transformed main character into a humanoid Necromancer on foot: 8-way WASD walking/gliding, aiming scythe & spells toward cursor, Shadow Step mist dash (Space/Shift), flowing robes with glowing cyan soul eyes, ebony bone scythe, and orbiting arcane relics. Hardcore difficulty overhaul: reduced player HP to 120, Silver Cavalry gallop lance charges, High Inquisitor ranged Holy Smite bolts interceptable by minions, lethal melee damage, and Resurrection Game Over screen.
* **Gothic-Ascendance** (Tag: `Gothic-Ascendance`) — Comprehensive visual overhaul: beveled flagstone highway pavers, dynamic persistent tread tracks and charnel ruts, colossal horned demon ram with heavy caterpillar treads, rotating barded cavalry with couched tournament lances, volumetric drifting fog banks, floating soul wisps, cinematic edge vignette, and ornate gothic filigree HUD.
* **Soul-Unbound** (Tag: `Soul-Unbound`) — Eliminated first-frame spell HUD freeze, added mouse click and keyboard support with hover highlights to Level-Up drafting, surplus EXP multi-level queueing, and automatic canvas focus.
* **Soul-Harvester** (Tag: `Soul-Harvester`) — Soul EXP bar progression with 3-card rogue-lite level-up drafting, unlockable minion tiers summoned on keys 1–6, and Carrion Scavenger perk enabling minions to autonomously harvest ground corpses.
